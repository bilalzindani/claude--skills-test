About

I build the systems, then hand you the keys.

I'm Bilal Zindani, an AI automation engineer based in Karachi, working with SMB owners across the US, Australia and the Gulf. Before automation I spent two years as a designer and shipped 100+ projects — which is why the systems I build look as sharp as they run.

Now I install AI operating systems: the voice agent that answers every call, the workflows that kill manual data entry, and the CRM that follows up before you'd even remember to. Not enterprise slideware — working systems, wired into the tools you already use.

If a task is repetitive, rule-based, or slipping through the cracks, it can probably run itself. My job is to make that happen — cleanly, and without you babysitting it.
